Why this matters

It now takes just a few seconds of recording — a YouTube video, an old voice note — to clone someone's voice convincingly. Fraud calls impersonating a son, a boss, or a relative are rising across the country, and most people simply can't tell a real voice from a cloned one over the phone.

One quick check can prevent a costly mistake.

  • Call back yourself. Use the saved number to confirm, not the number the call came from.
  • Keep a family safe word. A code word only your household knows, to ask for in an emergency call.
  • Don't send money instantly. Scammers create urgency on purpose — take a minute to verify.
  • Report it. Report suspected fraud to the 1930 helpline or at cybercrime.gov.in.
This is a prototype tool that gives an estimate based on audio patterns, not a 100% guarantee. If you're facing or suspect a financial fraud, call 1930 (National Cyber Crime Helpline) right away or report it at cybercrime.gov.in.
